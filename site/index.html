It reads the contract
before you sign.

When a website asks you to approve something, the wallet stops and says what it is.

The three verdicts, from the app.

What it tells you.

  • What the call does, in plain words. Unlimited approvals and "approve all" are named.
  • Whether the contract, and the address getting the right to spend, have public source code.
  • Whether the contract is on a short list of audited ones. If not, it says so.
  • Unknown contracts keep the approve button locked until you tick that you read it. Approving then asks for your fingerprint, face or passcode.

Public source code means anyone can read it. It does not mean it is safe. If you are not sure, reject.

Yours, and only yours.
